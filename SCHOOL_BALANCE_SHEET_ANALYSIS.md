### 🟡 POTENTIAL ENHANCEMENTS (Optional)

#### 1. **More Detailed Breakdowns**
Current: Shows all receivables together  
Enhancement: Could add sub-categories:
- Student fees receivable (by grade/program)
- Staff advances
- Other debtors

**Recommendation:** Current structure is fine. Too much detail clutters the balance sheet. Details should be in subsidiary ledgers.

#### 2. **Aging Analysis**
Current: Shows total receivables  
Enhancement: Could show:
- Current (0-30 days)
- 30-60 days overdue
- 60-90 days overdue
- Over 90 days overdue

**Recommendation:** This is better suited for a separate "Aged Receivables Report" rather than the balance sheet. Balance sheet should show net realizable value.

#### 3. **Restricted vs Unrestricted Funds**
Current: Shows total equity  
Enhancement: For non-profit schools:
- Unrestricted funds (operating)
- Temporarily restricted (designated donations)
- Permanently restricted (endowments)

**Recommendation:** Only needed for non-profit schools with donor restrictions. For-profit schools don't need this.

## Sample School Balance Sheet Interpretation

### Example: Reading Your School's Balance Sheet

```
ASSETS                                  2025
─────────────────────────────────────────────
Fixed Assets (School Building, Furniture)    50,000,000 FCFA
Inventory (Books, Supplies)                    2,000,000 FCFA
Student Fees Receivable                       15,000,000 FCFA ⚠️
Cash & Banks                                   8,000,000 FCFA
─────────────────────────────────────────────
TOTAL ASSETS                                  75,000,000 FCFA

LIABILITIES & EQUITY
─────────────────────────────────────────────
School Capital                                40,000,000 FCFA
Retained Earnings                              5,000,000 FCFA
Advance Tuition Payments                      20,000,000 FCFA 📝
Teachers' Salaries Payable                     8,000,000 FCFA
Suppliers Payable                              2,000,000 FCFA
─────────────────────────────────────────────
TOTAL LIABILITIES & EQUITY                    75,000,000 FCFA ✅
```

### What This Tells You:

1. **Total School Worth:** 75 million FCFA
2. **School Equity:** 45 million FCFA (Capital + Retained Earnings)
3. **Students Owe You:** 15 million FCFA (need to collect!)
4. **You Owe Students:** 20 million FCFA (prepaid next term fees)
5. **Available Cash:** 8 million FCFA (for operations)
6. **Owe Teachers:** 8 million FCFA (must pay salaries)

### Key Insights:
- ⚠️ **High Receivables:** 15M in outstanding fees - need collection drive
- 📝 **Good Prepayments:** 20M advance payments - healthy cash flow
- ✅ **Balanced Sheet:** Assets = Liabilities (accounting is correct)
- 💰 **Liquidity Check:** 8M cash vs 10M short-term payables (8M salaries + 2M suppliers) = Need to collect some receivables soon!
