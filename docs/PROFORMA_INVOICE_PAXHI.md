## **SYSTEM MODULES & FEATURES**

### **1. Dashboard & Analytics**
*   **Centralized Dashboard:** Real-time overview of system metrics, student statistics, and financial summaries.

### **2. Admission & Student Management**
*   **Admission System:** Online application processing, admission fee configuration, and automated student registration.
*   **Student Lifecycle:** Comprehensive student profiles, transfer management (in/out), and alumni tracking.
*   **Enrollment:** Flexible course enrollment (single/group), subject add/drop, and course completion tracking.
*   **ID Cards:** Automated ID card generation and printing.

### **3. Academic Administration**
*   **Structure Management:** Management of Faculties, Departments, Programs, Batches, Sessions, and Semesters.
*   **Course Management:** Subject creation, section allocation, and classroom management.
*   **Routine Management:** Automated class and exam routine generation, teacher schedules, and joint class scheduling.

### **4. Class Hub & Digital Logbook**
*   **Digital Logbook:** Automated recording of teaching activities, replacing physical logbooks.
*   **Session Tracking:** Real-time tracking of class sessions, topics covered, and teaching duration.
*   **Class Kiosk:** Interface for lecturers to start sessions and mark attendance (QR/Manual).
*   **Hub Reports:** Detailed analytics on syllabus coverage, teacher punctuality, and session completion.

### **5. Examination & Grading**
*   **Exam Management:** Exam scheduling, attendance tracking, and admit card generation.
*   **Grading System:** Configurable grading scales, subject marking, and result processing.
*   **Transcripts & Certificates:** Automated generation of semester marksheets, final transcripts, and certificates.

### **6. Financial Management (Fees & Accounting)**
*   **Fee Collection:** Student fee invoicing, payment collection, installment tracking, and penalty management.
*   **OHADA Accounting:** Full double-entry accounting system including Chart of Accounts, General Ledger, Journal Entries, and Trial Balance.
*   **Budgeting:** Budget planning, variance analysis, and cashflow projections.
*   **Income & Expense:** Tracking of all institutional revenue and operational expenses.
*   **Platform Fees:** Management of platform usage fees and exemptions.

### **7. Human Resource & Payroll**
*   **Staff Management:** Digital staff profiles, designation management, and document storage.
*   **Attendance & Leave:** Biometric/manual attendance tracking and leave application workflows.
*   **Payroll:** Automated salary processing, payslip generation, tax calculations, and allowance/deduction management.

### **8. Library & E-Library**
*   **Physical Library:** Book inventory, issue/return tracking, and member management.
*   **E-Library:** Digital repository for e-books and resources with AI-powered recommendations.
*   **Smart Features:** Barcode integration and overdue penalty calculation.

### **9. Inventory Management**
*   **Stock Control:** Item categorization, stock tracking, and store management.
*   **Procurement:** Supplier management and item issuance tracking.

### **10. Communication & Collaboration**
*   **Messaging:** Integrated Email and SMS notification systems.
*   **Events & Notices:** Digital notice board and event calendar management.
*   **Study Materials:** Distribution of assignments and digital content to students.

### **11. Web Portal Management**
*   **CMS:** Full content management for the institution's public website (News, Gallery, Events, Testimonials, FAQ).
*   **Institutional Info:** Management of About Us, Leadership Team, History Timeline, and Accreditations.
*   **Academic Showcase:** Display of Courses, Campus Life, and Student Projects.
*   **Admissions Portal:** Management of Admission Dates, Requirements, and Online Application Pages.
*   **Front-End Control:** Customization of Sliders, Topbar, Social Media Links, and Call-to-Action sections.

### **12. Security & Administration**
*   **Access Control:** Granular Role-Based Access Control (RBAC) for all user types.
*   **Security Suite:** IP whitelisting, blocked IP management, and security dashboards.
*   **Audit Trail:** Comprehensive logging of all user activities for accountability.
*   **System Settings:** Global configuration, localization, and backup management.

### **13. Reporting Intelligence**
*   **Academic Reports:** Student progress, attendance, and course performance reports.
*   **Financial Reports:** Fee collection, income/expense, and payroll reports.
*   **Operational Reports:** Library usage, inventory status, and audit logs.

### **14. Student Portal Features**
*   **Academic Dashboard:** Personalized view of class routines, exam schedules, and real-time results.
*   **Course Management:** Online course registration, add/drop subjects, and access to study materials/assignments.
*   **Financial Center:** View fee status, make online payments, track payment plans, and download receipts.
*   **Class Hub Access:** Join live classes, view session history, and access digital notes.
*   **E-Library Integration:** Browse digital resources, receive AI book recommendations, and track reading insights.
*   **Administrative Tools:** Apply for leave, request transcripts, register for resits, and download official forms (A2/A3).
