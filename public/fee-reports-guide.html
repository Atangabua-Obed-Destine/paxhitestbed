How to Access

Collected Fees Report

View all collected fees with payment tracking

  1. Login to Admin Portal
  2. Go to Reports menu
  3. Click Collected Fees
  4. Select filters and Payment Status
  5. Click Search
Open Report
Student Fees Report

View individual student payment history

  1. Login to Admin Portal
  2. Go to Reports menu
  3. Click Student Fees
  4. Select a Student
  5. Choose Payment Status filter
  6. Click Search
Open Report
